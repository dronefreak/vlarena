# Code of Conduct

vlarena adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion
focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are
not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, dronefreak, at
[kumaar324@gmail.com](mailto:kumaar324@gmail.com) ([@dronefreak](https://github.com/dronefreak)). Reports are handled confidentially,
and the maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban contributors,
for behaviour that violates this code.
